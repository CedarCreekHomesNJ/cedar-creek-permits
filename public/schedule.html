How this works. Every task carries predecessors, a length in working days, and a notice period in calendar days. A forward pass from what you've marked complete gives each job its projected CO date and critical path; eight rules then decide what reaches this screen. Change one status and everything recomputes.
Defaults. Notice periods and task lengths fall back to 7 days unless your sheet specified otherwise or you've set them on the task. Township inspections are excluded from the length default, since their real elapsed time is the township's turnaround.
Contractors. Each job carries its own crew across 22 trades, set on the job and overridable on any single task. Mark a task complete and you'll get the hand-off — who to contact next, and by when.
What each screen shows. Today is a triage list — only tasks that trip one of the rules reach it, so a task that is on schedule and not yet due will not appear. The Job Sheet is the complete record: use the Open / Complete / All filters or the search box to reach any of the 149 tasks, including finished ones. Trades and the look-ahead cover the next six weeks on open jobs only; closed jobs drop out of both.
